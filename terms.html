Lux (Blair Forest) SMS Terms

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Lux is a private family assistant operated by Joel Blair. This service is not open to the public.

Program

Lux sends text messages such as reminders, schedule and errand updates, shared notes and links, and replies to texts you send in. Recipients are the owner and family members who have personally asked to receive messages. Message frequency varies, about 20 per month or fewer.

Opt-in and opt-out

You are added only after you ask the owner to receive messages. Reply STOP at any time to end all messages. Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy. Mobile numbers are not shared with third parties for marketing.
